Sherlog privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Sherlog is a Chrome extension that helps diagnose failed scheduled processes in Oracle Fusion. This page explains what data it handles, why, and who processes it.

What Sherlog reads

Sherlog runs only on Oracle Fusion pages (*.oraclecloud.com). On the Scheduled Processes screen it reads, for each row, the request ID, process name and status so it can show a diagnose icon. It does not read other pages, your browsing history, passwords or Fusion credentials.

What is sent, and when

Nothing leaves your browser until you press Diagnose or rate an answer. Then:

  • The request ID, process name and status of that one process are sent to the Sherlog backend.
  • The backend fetches that process's log and output from Oracle Fusion using its own service account. These can contain business data, such as journal, invoice or payment details, depending on the process.
  • With each request the backend records your IP address, browser user agent and the extension version, to limit request rates and to troubleshoot.
  • If you rate a diagnosis, your rating and any comment you type are saved with it.

Why it is used

Only to diagnose the scheduled process you asked about, and to improve the quality of future diagnoses by reviewing past answers and their ratings. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or anything unrelated to this purpose.

Who processes it

  • The Sherlog backend, operated by us, which stores diagnoses, ratings and the details above in its database.
  • AI models reached through OpenRouter, which receive the process log and output to produce the diagnosis. They act as service providers for this purpose only.

We do not sell or share this data with anyone else.

What is stored in your browser

Only Sherlog's settings (the backend address) and your preferred panel width. You can clear them by removing the extension.

How long it is kept

Diagnoses, ratings and request details are kept for 12 months and then deleted. You can ask for data linked to your requests to be deleted sooner by contacting us.
